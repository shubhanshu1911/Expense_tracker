Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed version of your password. When you log expenses, we store the details you provide: amount, category, date, and description.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and authenticating your account. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside the core app functionality.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored on our servers. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms before being saved. We take reasonable precautions to protect your data from unauthorized access.

You can delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated expense data and personal information will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We do not integrate with external analytics providers, advertising networks, or data brokers.

We use Google Fonts to load typography assets. Your browser may make a request to Google's servers when loading the app; this is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or would like to request deletion of your data, please reach out via the contact information provided in the app.
